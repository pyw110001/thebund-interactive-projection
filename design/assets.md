# Visual assets and generation

The accepted concept was generated with the built-in Image Gen tool and copied to `approved-concept.png`. Architectural geometry is governed by the Rhino model rather than by the concept render.

`public/assets/floral-garden.png` is a standalone Image Gen artwork. Original output size: 2128 × 739. The default artwork uses a centered vertical crop in UV space to fill the 39.6 × 5.4m screen. The original asset remains unchanged. User-selected media uses the chosen contain / cover mode.

Generation prompt:

> Create a production texture asset, an extremely WIDE PANORAMIC digital flower garden artwork for a 40 meter long rear projection screen. Desired aspect ratio about 7.3:1, use the widest supported horizontal canvas, fill edge to edge with artwork. No screen, no architecture, no text, no typography, no border, no black bars, no UI. Detailed exquisite ethereal floral landscape, art installation like a continuous living tapestry. Low undulating clouds of thousands of tiny blossoms, feathery pink grasses, pale cream cosmos, white daisies, lilac lavender, soft coral blossoms and periwinkle flowers, layered low mounds, beautifully detailed distinct petals and stems. Pastel ivory peach pale lilac background occupying upper third, a hazy distant low lavender hill, gentle depth, NO large trees, no mountains, no giant oversized flowers. Foreground dense flowers across entire lower edge. Composition stays horizontal across the full width, tonal color clusters progress from warm ivory blush at left through lavender in center to soft peach pink at right. Soft natural diffuse lighting, no neon, no oversaturation, gallery quality realistic CGI botanical art with delicate material textures. Intended for projected digital art so bright readable midtones, subtle shadows, crisp fine texture. Single seamless-feeling panoramic composition. Only the standalone flower artwork.

The 512px paving material and low planting detail are deterministic procedural scene assets generated by `build_blender.py`, using seed 47. Existing planting-bed boundaries come from the Rhino source; no extra flower sculptures are introduced.
